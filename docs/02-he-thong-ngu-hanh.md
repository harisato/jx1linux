# He Thong Ngu Hanh - Vo Lam Truyen Ky 1

## Tong quan

Net dac sac cua Vo Lam Truyen Ky phu thuoc vao yeu to ngu hanh tuong sinh - tuong khac.
Thap dai mon phai vi the duoc phan chia theo 5 hanh: Kim, Moc, Thuy, Hoa, Tho.

## Ngu Hanh Tuong Sinh

Tuong Sinh la moi quan he ho tro lan nhau, giup do nhau cung phat trien.

```
Moc -> Hoa -> Tho -> Kim -> Thuy -> Moc
```

Chi tiet giai thich:
- **Moc sinh Hoa**: Cay kho sinh ra lua, Hoa lay Moc lam nguyen lieu dot
- **Hoa sinh Tho**: Lua dot chay moi thu thanh tro bui, tro bui vun dap thanh dat
- **Tho sinh Kim**: Kim loai, quang hinh thanh tu trong dat
- **Kim sinh Thuy**: Kim loai neu bi nung chay o nhiet do cao se tao ra dung dich the long
- **Thuy sinh Moc**: Nuoc duy tri su song cua cay

## Ngu Hanh Tuong Khac

Tuong Khac co nghia la ap che lan nhau.

```
Moc -> Tho -> Thuy -> Hoa -> Kim -> Moc
```

Chi tiet:
- **Moc khac Tho**: Re cay am tham hap thu chat dinh duong tu dat
- **Tho khac Thuy**: Dat hut nuoc, co the ngan chan dong chay cua nuoc
- **Thuy khac Hoa**: Nuoc dap tat lua
- **Hoa khac Kim**: Lua nung chay kim loai
- **Kim khac Moc**: Kim loai (dao, riu) chat ha cay

## Ngu Hanh trong Trang Bi

Thuoc tinh Ngu hanh duoc ap dung trong cach trang bi quan ao, vu khi, nhan, non...
Khong phai co gi mac do ma phai mac sao cho tot va hieu qua.

### Quy tac mac do Ngu Hanh (5 Hanh)
Muc dich: Kich hoat cac dong an cua trang bi de toi uu hoa tinh nang va suc manh.

Quy tac chung:
- Mon phai he Moc tu kich dong he Hoa
- Mon phai he Hoa tu kich dong he Tho
- Mon phai he Tho tu kich dong he Kim
- Mon phai he Kim tu kich dong he Thuy
- Mon phai he Thuy tu kich dong he Moc

### Vi du cu the
Neu ban choi Thieu Lam (he Kim):
- Mac do he Thuy de kich hoat dong an (vi Kim sinh Thuy)
- Hoac mac do he Tho (vi Tho sinh Kim)
- Tranh mac do he Hoa (vi Hoa khac Kim)

## Ngu Hanh trong Chien Dau (PK)

### Chien luoc PK theo ngu hanh:
- **Uu tien danh phai bi khac**: Vi du Thieu Lam (Kim) nen de Ngu Doc, Duong Mon (Moc) ma danh
- **Tranh ne phai khac minh**: Vi du Thieu Lam nen ne Cai Bang, Thien Nhan (Hoa)
- **Doi hinh Tong Kim/Cong Thanh**: Can phan bo deu cac he de tuong ho

### Sat thuong ngu hanh:
- Khi tan cong doi phuong bi khac he, sat thuong tang them % nhat dinh
- Khi bi doi phuong khac he tan cong, chiu them sat thuong
- Vi vay trang bi khang ngu hanh rat quan trong

## Ngu Hanh trong San Boss

He thong Ngu Hanh tuong khac cuc ky quan trong trong viec san Boss:
- So huu doi hinh hop ly (co nhan vat he khac boss) se giup viec san Boss tro nen nhanh gon
- Boss thuong co he ngu hanh co dinh, can chuan bi doi hinh phu hop
- Vi du boss he Hoa thi nen mang nhan vat he Thuy de tang sat thuong
